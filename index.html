Questions

Short answers to what people ask first.

Is Grox a real bank or payment app?

No. Grox is a simulation. There are no real accounts, no real money and no real card details. Nothing you enter can move funds.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device. Grox has no sign-up and does not send your transactions to a server.

What if I delete the app or clear my browser data?

Your saved data can be lost with it. Use the backup option now and then so you can restore it later.

Should I enter my real card number?

No, please don't. Use made-up names and numbers. Grox does not need real financial details to work.

Can I change the currency or colors?

Yes. Choose from nine themes and seven currencies in the settings.

Ready to look at your money differently?

Practice, explore and stay in control. No account needed.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: Grox Wallet is a simulation. It has no accounts and handles no real money. Your data stays on your device, and we do not collect it.

1. What Grox is

Grox Wallet is a practice app for tracking and analyzing example transactions. It is not a bank, payment service or financial institution. It cannot send, receive or hold real funds.

2. Information you enter

You may enter things such as transaction names, amounts, card nicknames, sample card numbers, your chosen theme and currency, and a PIN. This information is saved on your device only. We do not receive a copy.

3. Please do not enter real financial details

Grox does not need real card numbers, bank details or passwords. Use made-up values. If you enter real details anyway, they stay on your device, but we recommend against it.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, email address or phone number.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts or photos.

5. Backups

The backup feature creates a file that you control. Where you save or send that file is your choice. Anyone who gets the file can read its contents, so store it somewhere safe.

6. Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. We do not control that.

7. Security

Your data is protected by your device and, if you turn it on, your PIN. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and clearing app or browser data deletes your information.

8. Children

Grox contains no real transactions and collects no personal information. Younger users should still use it with a parent or guardian's guidance.

9. Your choices

You can delete your data at any time by removing the app or clearing its stored data. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you. That is why we suggest making a backup.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If Grox ever adds real accounts or collects data, we will update this page first.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at groxwallet@outlook.com.
